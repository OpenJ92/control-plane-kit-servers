## Governing laws and risks

Draft head, selected revision and prepared revision remain distinct. Exact-key
retry resolves historical intent; a fresh preparation of the same selected
revision with fresh fences is how CPK can establish a converged no-op.

The client journal owns bounded private transport references and exact replay
material. CPK owns durable sessions, actions, revisions, plans and execution.
The client must expose partial session history rather than invent atomicity,
cleanup or successful completion after response loss.

Reports compose bounded authenticated public observations. They do not turn
local receipts into workflow authority or claim an atomic global snapshot.
Missing, inconsistent and uncertain evidence remains explicit.

Security: existing credential-file roles, authenticated public commands, exact
plan/destructive approval, redaction and bounded output remain mandatory.
This roadmap setup authorizes no provider mutation or live teardown. A later
live acceptance needs its actual inspectable plans and appropriate permission.
Servers #123 remains the owner of generic convergence acceptance; no duplicate
scenario matrix is planned.
